World news — Wednesday, 11 November 2026.

US-mediated talks between Ukraine and Russia ended their second day in Abu Dhabi without a final peace deal, but the two sides agreed in principle to exchange about 1,000 prisoners of war each (Reuters). Ukraine said its forces damaged five oil storage tanks, with a combined capacity of 150,000 cubic metres, at Russia's Baltic port of Ust-Luga; Russian strikes hit six Ukrainian power substations and a market in Chornomorsk, killing three people and injuring 11 (Ukrainian General Staff; regional officials). NATO jets were scrambled after drones entered Baltic airspace.

In the Middle East, Saudi Arabia said it intercepted seven Houthi missiles and drones, including cruise missiles aimed at Riyadh (Saudi officials). Israel swore in a new parliament after a deadlocked election left Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu without a majority and no government formed (Reuters).

At the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres warned that the slow response to climate change had put the world "on a path to destruction" (United Nations). The US Justice Department charged three alleged Iran-backed operatives over nearly 20 plots in the US and Europe, including a September plot at RAF Fairford in Britain, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved to a military base after threats (Justice Department; New York Times).

Democrat Scott Colom won a US Senate seat in Mississippi, defeating incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith (Associated Press). British police arrested a US Army officer in England on suspicion of spying for Russia. Brent crude settled at $92.76 a barrel, up 1.39%, and the Nasdaq rose 1.6% on artificial-intelligence shares (Reuters). Anthropic is seeking up to $3.5 billion from Japanese investors ahead of a possible $1 trillion initial public offering, according to reports. In New Delhi, air stayed poor after Diwali, with the average air-quality index at 299 on Wednesday morning.
